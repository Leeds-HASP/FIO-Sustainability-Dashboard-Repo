Sustainable Food Sales Explorer

A dashboard to explore seasonal variations in greenhouse gas emissions, land use and water use from food and drink sales.

User Quick-Start Guide

This dashboard shows the environmental impact of food and drink purchases across a calendar year (2022) from a major UK supermarket retailer. Sales from loyalty card holders living in Yorkshire and the Humber region were linked to product-level environmental sustainability estimates to show how the carbon, land use, and water use footprints of all purchases change over time and across different food categories.

About the Metrics

The dashboard allows picking from four different environmental metrics:

  • GHGE = Greenhouse Gas Emissions (kg CO2-equivalent)
  • LU = Land Use (agricultural land area [m²] multiplied by length of time occupied [years])
  • WU = Water Use (agricultural fresh water withdrawals in litres)
  • A Composite Impact score, calculated by scaling each of the above metrics between 0 and 1 and calculating the normalised average of the three scaled metrics. The Composite Impact score gives an indication of overall environmental impact, weighting GHGE, LU and WU equally.

Environmental impact data were derived from Poore and Nemechek's global averages for food and drink commodities [1], and were linked to products as described here [2]. The system boundaries are from cradle to store gate.

Dashboard Controls

  • Metric (Dropdown): Switch between GHGE, LU, WU, and Composite.
  • Total SF / Per kg (Toggle): Switch between volume-derived footprint and per-kg composition.
  • Date Range (Date pickers): Select start and end dates to zoom into a window.
  • Reset Date (Button): Return to the full date range.
  • N categories (Dropdown): Number of categories shown in the heatmap.

Suggested Steps to Get Started

  1. Select a metric from the dropdown to update the chart.
  2. Toggle between Total SF and Per kg to switch views.
  3. Use the From and To date pickers to narrow to a period of interest.
  4. Hover on any data point, marker, or cell to read exact values.
  5. Use the event overlay dropdown to mark key dates.
  6. Reset dates returns to the full year view.

Detailed Usage Instructions

How can this dashboard be used?

The dashboard has two charts, which allow both a high-level overview of environmental footprint trends across a full year, as well as exploration of environmental footprints down to individual food categories.

  1. TIMELINE CHART (Overall Seasonal Patterns tab): Shows one smoothed trend line across the year per metric, rising when purchasing was more environmentally intensive. The line shows the 7-day smoothed environmental footprint across the selected period. A peak means purchasing was more intensive on those days.

    The chart can be configured as follows:
    • Choose Metric: Select a metric (e.g. GHGE, LU etc.) using the dropdown.
    • Choose Unit: Switch between Total SF and Per kg. Total SF tells us the total environmental impact from product sales, which depends on the types of products purchased, and the volumes in which they are purchased. Per kg footprints tells us about the mix or composition of products purchased, and whether this mix is more or less sustainable. This metric controls for the total volume of products purchased, which will vary across the year (e.g. increasing at Christmas).
    • Choose Date Range: Narrow the date range using ‘From’ and ‘To’ date pickers to zoom into any period. The y-axis rescales automatically. For the Composite metric the y-axis always starts from zero so Total SF and Per kg remain directly comparable.
    • Explore Values: Hover anywhere to snap to the nearest data point.
    • Event Overlay: Use the drop-down list to select different events to overlay on the graph (e.g. public holidays, school holidays, or key weather events).

  2. HEATMAP CHART (Category-level Patterns tab): Shows how the environmental impact of individual food categories varies across the year, revealing category-level seasonal trends. Each row is one food category, each column is one week. The colour of a cell shows how that category's average daily footprint over the week compared to its own typical level for the year. Darker indicates the footprint is above average for that row. The scale is per row (i.e. category) so colour cannot be used to compare magnitude across rows (i.e. categories).

    The chart can be configured as follows:
    • Choose Metric and Unit: Select a metric (e.g. GHGE, LU etc.) using the dropdown at the top of the chart. Use the Total SF or Per kg toggle to change what the colours represent. The row (i.e. category) order updates to reflect the most impactful categories for the active metric and unit combination.
    • Choose Number of Categories: Set how many categories to show using the top N dropdown.
    • Explore Data
      • Rank: Annual rankings for the selected environmental impact metric can be found to the right of the heatmap. The rankings tell you how the category's annual footprint compares to all other categories (1 = highest, 30 = lowest environmental impact).
      • Annual mean SF: This metric is found in the top right of the chart. It shows the mean daily sales footprint for the chosen environmental impact metric (e.g. GHGE) across all products sold.
      • Annual mean per kg: As above, but per kg, rather than total sales footprint.
    • Hover on any cell to read the week's footprint statistics:
      • Week's Rank: tells you how the week's footprint ranks compared to all other categories for that week (1 = highest, 30 = lowest environmental impact).
      • Deviation from the annual mean: tells you how the week's footprint differs from the average footprint for that category across the year.
      • Annual contribution to total SF: this statistic only shows when Total SF is selected. It gives the percentage contribution for a given category to the total sales footprint across all products.

How to use the Timeline chart

  • Choose Metric: Select GHGE, LU, WU, or Composite using the dropdown.
  • Choose Unit: Toggle between Total SF (volume and composition combined) and Per kg (composition only, controls for total volume purchased).
  • Choose Date Range: Use From and To date pickers to zoom into any period. The y-axis rescales automatically.
  • Explore Values: Hover anywhere on the chart to snap to the nearest data point and read the value, annual mean, and deviation.
  • Event Overlay: Use the dropdown to overlay public holidays, school terms, weather events, or sporting events.

How to use the Heatmap chart

  • Choose Metric and Unit: Select a metric and toggle Total SF or Per kg. Row order updates to reflect the most impactful categories. Per kg view uses a diverging colour scale.
  • Choose Number of Categories: Set how many categories to show using the top N dropdown (default 30).
  • Rank: Annual rankings shown to the right of the heatmap (1 = highest impact).
  • Hover a cell to read the week's rank, deviation from the annual mean, and annual contribution to total SF.
  • Event Overlay: Use the dropdown to mark public holidays, school terms, or other events above the chart.

  • Reset Date (Button): Reset date period to go back to default (full date range in data, entire calendar year).
  • N categories (Dropdown): Number of categories to show (Top-N categories ranked for environmental impact. Options include 10, 30, or 65 categories at a time. Heatmap chart only).

  5. Use the drop-down to select events of interest (e.g. public holidays).
  6. Reset dates returns both charts to the full year view (no change to other inputs).
